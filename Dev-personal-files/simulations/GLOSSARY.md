# Samar Island Agri-Feeds Trading — Domain Glossary

This glossary defines the operational vocabulary of a feed distribution business operating across Samar Island, with a main warehouse in Catbalogan City and a satellite depot in Catarman.

## Locations & Logistics

**Main Bodega**:
The primary warehouse and walk-in retail counter in Catbalogan City that receives mill shipments, stores bulk inventory, and dispatches route trucks.
_Avoid_: HQ, central store

**Satellite Depot**:
A secondary counter and storage location in Catarman that serves Northern Samar customers and receives stock replenishments from the Main Bodega.
_Avoid_: branch office, outpost

**Delivery Truck**:
A company-owned cargo truck that hauls pre-ordered customer deliveries, depot replenishments, and rolling stock along Samar routes.
_Avoid_: delivery van, carrier

**Northbound Route**:
The scheduled delivery circuit running north from Catbalogan through Gandara and Calbayog to the Catarman Satellite Depot and back.
_Avoid_: trip, run

**Stock Transfer (`STF`)**:
The internal movement of inventory from the Main Bodega to the Satellite Depot without a sale or change in ownership.
_Avoid_: relocation, internal sale

**Rolling Stock**:
Unallocated sacks loaded onto a Delivery Truck above confirmed pre-orders for opportunistic ex-truck sales along the route, with any unsold sacks returned at end of day.
_Avoid_: extra load, van buffer, free stock

## Inventory & Units

**Sealed Sack**:
An unopened factory-packed 50 kg or 25 kg bag of feed or raw ingredient as received from the supplier.
_Avoid_: bag, unit

**Sack-Breaking**:
The act of opening a Sealed Sack at the counter to transfer its contents into a Loose Bin for per-kilogram retail selling.
_Avoid_: splitting, unpacking, repackaging

**Loose Bin**:
An open retail container holding weighed, per-kilogram feed taken from a broken Sealed Sack.
_Avoid_: open sack, retail scoop

**Expiry Lot**:
A specific supplier production batch identified by its lot code, manufacture date, and expiration date.
_Avoid_: batch number, shipment

**FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out)**:
The picking rule requiring that the Expiry Lot with the earliest expiration date be dispatched or sold before newer lots of the same product.
_Avoid_: FIFO when referring to shelf-life picking

**Short Delivery**:
An inbound supplier shipment whose delivered quantity is less than the quantity ordered on the Purchase Order.
_Avoid_: missing stock, shrinkage

## Pricing, Tax & Trade Documents

**Price Tier**:
A customer pricing schedule—Walk-In Retail, Farm, or Dealer—that determines the unit selling price for each product.
_Avoid_: price list, customer group

**Volume Rebate**:
A per-sack price reduction granted when a customer purchases at or above a qualifying sack quantity on an order.
_Avoid_: promo, markdown

**VAT-Exempt Agricultural Feed**:
Animal feed or raw feed ingredient exempt from Philippine 12% Value-Added Tax under agricultural feed rules.
_Avoid_: tax-free item

**VATable Veterinary Supplement**:
A veterinary medicine, vitamin, or electrolyte product subject to 12% Philippine Value-Added Tax.
_Avoid_: taxable add-on

**Purchase Order (`PO`)**:
A formal order placed with a supplier specifying products, quantities, and agreed unit costs for future delivery.

**Receiving Report (`RR`)**:
An internal warehouse record confirming the exact quantities and Expiry Lots physically received against a supplier shipment.

**Delivery Receipt (`DR`)**:
A transport and handoff document listing the items and quantities loaded onto a truck and signed by the customer upon delivery.

**Sales Invoice (`SI`)**:
A commercial billing document issued for a cash or immediate-settlement sale.

**Charge Invoice (`CI`)**:
A commercial billing document issued for a credit sale payable at a later due date.

**Collection Receipt (`CR`)**:
A payment receipt issued when a customer settles or partially pays an open Charge Invoice via cash, bank transfer, GCash, or check.

**Check/Payment Voucher (`CV`)**:
An internal disbursement record documenting payment made to a supplier for an open invoice or COD purchase.

## Payments & Credit

**Credit Terms**:
The agreed number of days (such as 15 or 30 days) a credit-approved customer or the business has to settle an invoice.
_Avoid_: utang, due days

**Credit Limit**:
The maximum unpaid receivable balance a customer is permitted to carry before new credit releases are held.
_Avoid_: max balance

**COD (Cash on Delivery)**:
A payment arrangement requiring full settlement upon physical delivery of goods.

**Partial Payment**:
A payment that covers less than the full unpaid balance of an open Charge Invoice, leaving the remainder due.
_Avoid_: installment, deposit

**Post-Dated Check (`PDC`)**:
A check dated for a future clearing date, accepted on account and held until it can be deposited on or after that date.
_Avoid_: future check, dated cheque

**Remittance**:
The end-of-day turnover by the route driver of all collected cash, checks, payment proofs, and unsold Rolling Stock back to the Main Bodega.
_Avoid_: turnover, liquidation
